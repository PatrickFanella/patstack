# prepare-product-launch

Ground positioning and launch claims in the current product and sources. A launch plan or packet is a draft deliverable, not publishing or account-operation authority.

Select the matching procedure below and load only that procedure and its needed resources. Each procedure retains its own client, tool and invocation limits. Internal handoffs use the matching procedure below rather than reloading this entrypoint. A reference does not authorize delegation, publication, deployment or an unrelated mutation.

- **launch dossier**: Use when creating or refreshing a product marketing packet: brief, positioning, landing and channel copy, demo brief, campaign plan. Read [the procedure](references/base-workflow.md).
- **launch runway**: Launch planning for products, features and betas: Product Hunt, waitlists, early access, announcements, GTM checklists. Read [the procedure](references/modes/launch-runway/guide.md).

Load [companion procedures](references/skill-routes.md) only when their stated condition applies.
