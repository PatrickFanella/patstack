# Companion procedures

Load a companion only under its stated condition. Current authorization and client-native tool contracts remain authoritative. Read the source once; avoid cycles and do not switch the active profile for a one-off reference.

| Skill | Relationship | When to load it |
|---|---|---|
| `build-writing-voice-guide` | optional | When the marketing packet lacks a usable voice reference. |
| `write-and-adapt-copy` | optional | When the launch plan needs announcement, email or landing copy. |
| `improve-conversion-rates` | optional | When a launch or waitlist page needs conversion work. |
| `create-sales-materials` | optional | When the launch needs sales collateral or enablement. |
| `research-market-positioning` | optional | When the packet lacks evidence-backed positioning. |
| `plan-ad-campaigns` | optional | When the launch plan includes paid campaigns. |
