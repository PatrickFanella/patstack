# Marketing starter template

Copy this directory into a project's `docs/marketing/`. Copy the adjacent [prompt library](../prompts.md) with it if you want the drafting prompts, and update that link for the destination layout. This template can be used without any agent installation or paid service.

1. Complete [context.md](context.md) with the product owner. Replace every `{{FIELD}}` and retain explicit unknowns in internal notes.
2. Add dated product evidence and brand-source references. Check the actual offer, destination, and first-use journey.
3. Draft [packet.md](packet.md) from that context. Remove sections that do not fit the product or campaign.
4. Use the prompts to produce variants, then check every factual claim and final asset.
5. Name the campaign owner, choose dates and channels, and publish through the project's normal approval process.

If the project has a visual brand starter that produces layouts, tokens, and asset records, point this marketing kit at the resulting brand pack. Give each project its own voice, promise, audience, and demonstration; one product's palette, tone, and motifs belong to that product.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] A new reader can identify the product, audience, supported action, and next step from the brief.
- [ ] Every capability, price, result, quote, and comparison has a dated source or is removed.
- [ ] Planned or disabled features do not appear as available capabilities.
- [ ] Destinations, first-use journey, eligibility, account requirements, and offer are checked.
- [ ] The packet has one concrete demonstration and channel-specific copy.
- [ ] Artwork comes from the product's brand master; media rights, captions, and alt text are recorded.
- [ ] All public placeholders and internal editorial notes are removed.
- [ ] Launch owner, workload, measurement definitions, and review dates are set.

Link the completed kit from the project's documentation index. Keep source research separate from approved copy and keep a dated record when a claim changes.
