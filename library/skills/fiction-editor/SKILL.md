# fiction-editor

Choose the requested editing pass. Preserve the story's emotional function and author intent; representation review is evidence-aware advice, not a guarantee. Do not rewrite beyond the authorized scope.

Select the matching procedure below and load only that procedure and its needed resources. Each procedure retains its own client, tool and invocation limits. Internal handoffs use the matching procedure below rather than reloading this entrypoint. A reference does not authorize delegation, publication, deployment or an unrelated mutation.

- **fiction line edit**: Use when fiction or narrative prose has solid structure but reads flat: monotonous sentence rhythm, generic word choice or inconsistent voice. Read [the procedure](references/base-workflow.md).
- **character arc doctor**: Character arcs: design or fix positive, negative or flat arcs when characters feel static, change feels unearned, or the false belief to overturn is unclear. Read [the procedure](references/modes/character-arc-doctor/guide.md).
- **dialogue doctor**: Fiction dialogue: diagnose flat or wooden dialogue, characters who sound alike, or missing subtext, and suggest revisions. Read [the procedure](references/modes/dialogue-doctor/guide.md).
- **sensitivity read**: Check fiction for representation harm: stereotypes, harmful tropes, handling of sensitive subjects. Use when writing marginalized characters or difficult material, or for a sensitivity read. Read [the procedure](references/modes/sensitivity-read/guide.md).
- **punchline lab**: Humor diagnosis and repair. Use when jokes fall flat, humor feels forced, punchlines miss, or comedic writing needs systematic setup, misdirection and payoff work. Read [the procedure](references/modes/punchline-lab/guide.md).
- **cliche escape**: Replace a cliched story element with a fresher one that keeps its emotional function, moving from the statistical center to the edge. Use when a first idea feels generic or predictable. Read [the procedure](references/modes/cliche-escape/guide.md).
- **trope refresher**: Cliche fixes: make a generic story element, familiar trope or too-convenient protagonist orbit feel fresh. Read [the procedure](references/modes/trope-refresher/guide.md).
