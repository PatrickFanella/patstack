# story-workbench

Choose coaching versus co-writing before proceeding. Coaching generates no story prose or dialogue. Draft only when requested, preserve the writer's choices, and keep genre, premise and outline constraints explicit.

Select the matching procedure below and load only that procedure and its needed resources. Each procedure retains its own client, tool and invocation limits. Internal handoffs use the matching procedure below rather than reloading this entrypoint. A reference does not authorize delegation, publication, deployment or an unrelated mutation.

- **story diagnosis**: Diagnose what a story needs at any stage and route to one intervention. Use when a writer is stuck, the narrative feels broken, or asks what's wrong with their story. Read [the procedure](references/base-workflow.md).
- **story mentor**: Coach a writer on their own story through questions, diagnosis and frameworks, never writing prose, dialogue or narrative for them. Use when the writer wants guidance only. Read [the procedure](references/modes/story-mentor/guide.md).
- **story cowriter**: Co-write fiction with the writer: draft prose, dialogue and alternatives that build on their ideas, using Story Sense frameworks. Use when the writer wants generated content. Read [the procedure](references/modes/story-cowriter/guide.md).
- **premise forge**: Generate or strengthen story concepts genre-first: emotional core, then setting, characters, plot. Use when starting a story, brainstorming premises, or fixing an emotionally vague concept. Read [the procedure](references/modes/premise-forge/guide.md).
- **genre promise check**: Genre diagnosis: fix an unclear genre promise, misplaced elements or competing genres, or generate genre-specific elements from the Writing Excuses elemental genres. Read [the procedure](references/modes/genre-promise-check/guide.md).
- **draft unblocker**: Fiction drafting blocks: get a first draft moving when the outline is done but pages are not, writer's block hits, or progress has stalled. Read [the procedure](references/modes/draft-unblocker/guide.md).
- **outline to chapter**: Chapter drafting: turn a complete outline into a polished chapter through iterative editorial passes. Needs an outline; not for outlining or diagnosing a draft. Read [the procedure](references/modes/outline-to-chapter/guide.md).

Read [conditional companions](references/skill-routes.md) only when their stated condition applies.
