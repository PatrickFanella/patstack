# Companion procedures

Load a companion only under its stated condition. Current authorization and client-native tool contracts remain authoritative. Read the source once; avoid cycles and do not switch the active profile for a one-off reference.

| Skill | Relationship | When to load it |
|---|---|---|
| `improve-conversion-rates` | optional | When post-click page conversion is what limits ROAS or CPA. |
| `generate-and-edit-images` | optional | When the campaign needs rendered image ad creative. |
| `research-market-positioning` | optional | When audience or competitor research for targeting is missing. |
